Player Log

A read-only history of what changed a player's RP, supply limit and Fate Card hand, newest first. It helps the organiser find out why a total looks wrong.

Players tab

AOrganiser view, with entries

The section sits at the foot of the Players tab, under the player cards (shown here as one stub card). One player at a time. Entries are newest first. "Organiser" entries have a solid blood tag and a blood edge so a hand-made +/- is easy to spot.

BBattle steal: the other side's log

A steal writes on both logs. This is the winner's log (Ninth Muster) for the same battle as the loser's "RP 5 → 3" entry in A.

CLate joiner and a single entry

A player who joined after War Round 1 has "Joined at War Round N" as the opening entry. When there is nothing else, only the opening entry shows.

DEmpty

Nothing recorded yet for that player (for example the log was just switched on and no round has begun).

EEveryone else

Players who are not the campaign owner get no section at all: no heading, no empty box, no hint that it exists. The Players tab looks exactly as it does today.
