Supply and the roster

(existing supply text, unchanged)

Force Organisation. Every unit has a role: Character, Battleline, Infantry, Vehicle, Monster or Other. Each warband should field at least 1 Character and 2 Battleline, and no more of each role than its slots allow. Slots grow with your conquests: each Shrine held adds a Character slot, each Fortress a Battleline, each Hive City an Infantry, each Forge a Vehicle, each Refinery a Monster and each Spaceport (your Capital counts) an Other, up to +3 per role. Lose the ground and you lose the slot, and any units left over are over limit. This is the Reach's guidance, not a law: your group decides how strictly to hold each other to it. Your supply limit still caps total points.
