Narrative campaign · 500 → 2000 pts

The Varkesh Reach

A dying sub-sector, a rift that will not close, and every warband that came to pick at the bones. Small squads, hard-won scars, no heroes of legend. Your models earn their names here.

How it works, step by step

Read this out to the group, or copy it into the group chat.

  1. The settingThree years ago the Hollowgate rift tore open at the heart of the Varkesh Reach. The Imperium's grip collapsed. The system is a scatter of worlds, each split into provinces, and every faction has its own reason for being here. The more players, the more worlds.
  2. Build a 750 pt rosterName your warband. No Epic Heroes or named characters, ever. Your leaders are nobodies who might become somebody. Start with 5 Requisition Points (RP) and a Capital province on one of the outer worlds.
  3. The War RoundThe campaign is played in War Rounds. At the start of each, everyone draws a Fate Card. Then each player takes one turn, smallest empire first.
  4. Take a campaign actionOn your turn pick one: Conquer, Raid, Recuperate, Muster, Fortify or Scout. Conquer and Raid start a battle. The others build you up without fighting.
  5. Generate the battleThe province sets the size: an outpost is small, a hive city is big. Worlds closer to the rift multiply it. Then roll a Mission Twist like "no Toughness 10+" or "−1 to hit at range". Otherwise it's a normal game of 40k.
  6. Outcome and rewardsUnits gain XP and risk scars. Both sides earn RP and grow their supply limit. The winner takes the province. Then the next player takes their turn.
  7. Grow your empireSpread province by province across your world, seize a Spaceport, then jump along warp lanes to the next world. Inner worlds mean bigger games and two twists at once.
  8. The HollowgateOnce 60% of the Reach is claimed (or by War Round 8), the rift opens: a 2000 pt fight reached from the inner worlds. Whoever holds it at the end of a War Round wins the Reach.
No epic heroes. No unit with the Epic Hero keyword and no named character, even "counts-as". Your Characters are built from datasheets and named by you.

    Rules

    Everything the War Room does, written out so you can play it on paper too.

    The War Round

    1. MusterEvery player draws 1 Fate Card (hand limit 5).
    2. Income+1 RP for your Capital and each Hive City you hold. Refineries draw extra cards.
    3. Turn orderFewest provinces goes first. Break ties with a roll-off.
    4. Each player's turnChoose one campaign action. If it starts a battle, generate it, play it, and apply the outcome before the next player goes.
    5. End of roundIf someone holds the Hollowgate, they win the Reach. Otherwise start the next War Round.

    Can't get every game played in one evening? Fine. A War Round can stretch over a couple of weeks. Non-battle turns take a minute and can be done over the group chat.

    The Reach

    The system is charted for your group: one more world than players (3 to 7 worlds), each with 5 to 7 provinces. Every player starts with a Capital on an outer world. Inner worlds sit nearest the Hollowgate.

    Where you can attack

    1. OverlandAny province that borders one you hold on the same world.
    2. PlanetfallIf you hold a Spaceport or your Capital on a world, you can attack any province on a world linked to it by a warp lane.
    3. The riftOnce the Hollowgate opens, anyone holding a province on an inner world can attack it.
    4. No footholdA player with no provinces can attack any unclaimed province on an outer world.

    Battle size

    Province size × the world's orbit. A little outpost stays a small fight all campaign, but a hive city on an inner world is a 2000 pt war.

    OrbitMultiplierTwists

    If either player's supply limit is lower than the battle size, play at the lower number. An attacker with fewer provinces than the defender is the Underdog and gets +10% points.

    Mission Twists (D12)

    After each battle

    1. Award XP+1 to every unit that fought and was not destroyed. +1 per enemy unit it destroyed. +2 to one unit you Marked for Glory before the game.
    2. Roll for the fallenEvery unit destroyed rolls a D6. On a 1 it gains a Battle Scar. Characters roll on a 1–2.
    3. Requisition+1 RP to both players, +1 more to the winner. Bank up to 10.
    4. SupplyBoth players' supply limits grow by 150 pts.
    5. SpoilsThe winner draws 1 Fate Card. A Conquer winner takes the province. A Raid winner steals 1 RP and a random Fate Card.
    6. Underdog's gritAny player now holding the fewest provinces in the campaign draws 1 Fate Card.

    The Fate Deck

    One shared deck. Each player also adds their faction's cards to their own draws. Hand limit 5. Play up to 1 card per 500 pts in the battle (rounded down, minimum 1). Reveal a card when you use it, then discard it.

    House note. These are suggestions. Strike any card that feels too strong after a few games, and write new ones as the story grows.
