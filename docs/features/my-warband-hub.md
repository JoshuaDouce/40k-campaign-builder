# My Warband hub

Added 2026-10-25.

The Roster tab is now **My Warband** (same place in the tab bar, still `#roster`). It is each player's hub:

- Name, faction, provinces, battles and wins; total points against supply; RP.
- **Fate hand:** the cards with Play and Discard (moved from the Players tab), with the deal animation.
- **Spend Requisition:** Reinforcements (+200 supply, moved from the Players tab), Requisition the Deck,
  Field Surgeon, Promotion and Seasoned Recruits.
- Force Organisation, the units, the post-battle helper and the Hall of Heroes, as before.

Players see only their own warband. The campaign owner gets an **Acting for** picker to help someone without
their phone. Hands stay private: the owner acting for another player sees only the count.

The Players tab keeps names, factions, stats, battle history and the hand count, and points to My Warband for
cards and RP. No game-module or data changes.
