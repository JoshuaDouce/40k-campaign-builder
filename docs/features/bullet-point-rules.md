# Rules tab as bullet points

Added 2026-10-26.

The Rules tab's paragraphs are now short bullet lists (`ul.rl`), grouped under small subheads (`h4.rl-h`), so each rule
reads on its own line. Tables (actions, orbits, province and world types, twists, ranks, honours, scars, relics,
training, requisition) are unchanged.

- **The War Round** is five numbered steps (Income, Plan, Reveal, Play, Round end) plus four bullets.
- **Your warband** is split into "In Setup" and "From War Round 1", then Roles, slots (Force Organisation or
  Character slots, by toggle), Datasheet limit and Knights (when on), and Mustering.
- Flavour and repeats were cut: the Breach Team Seven example, the "heavy armour comes later" lines, and the note
  that Reinforcements is the only way supply grows.
- Rules still follow the owner's toggles, as before.
