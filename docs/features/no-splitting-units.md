# Units can be fielded smaller, never split

Added 2026-10-26. Replaces the old "slots hold models, not fixed units" rule (see `setup-slot-types.md`).

Rules tab, "Mustering for a battle":

- You can field a unit at a smaller size than it is on your roster.
- You can't split a unit: ten Intercessors on your roster take the field as one squad of up to ten, never two squads
  of five.
- Build each game's army from your roster with the normal 40k muster rules.

Text only: the app doesn't track which units are fielded in a game.
